## Description
I will be making a rain game that artist can use when they're wanting ideas on what they should draw.
They would use their cursor to catch any words that would fall and it would be added later for a complete idea.

## Features
- Digital Rain of words
	- I will use pygames to create a digital rain containing words related to art.
- Different art categories
	- I will use statements so when one category has all its words, it will move to the next one, ex: medium, tone, colors.
- Imported Libraries 
	- I will create my own libraries that will contain all the words needed for the rain.

## Challenges
- I will need to learn how to make the rain into text as oppose to just shapes.
- How to switch section when user has all the words for one section.
- How to make cursor interact and click on the words for that information to be returned.

## Outcomes
Ideal Outcome:
- Briefly describe what is the ideal outcome?
- The ideal outcome would be that the rain successfully falls down at the precise speed, it returns all the data from each section, then compiles it into an idea.
Minimal Viable Outcome:
- For the rain to fall down, all the words are selected, then put it together.
